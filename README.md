# 🚀 TeachFlow AI

### Less Searching. More Teaching.

> An AI-powered Educational Resource Discovery Agent that helps teachers find curriculum-aligned videos, simulations, worksheets, diagrams, PDFs, and learning resources in seconds.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Teachers and students spend too much time searching and too little time learning.

When a teacher wants to teach a topic like **Magnetism**, **Photosynthesis**, or **How AI Works**, they often have to search across:

* YouTube
* Google
* DIKSHA
* NCERT
* Khan Academy
* PhET Simulations
* Worksheets & PDFs
* Educational Websites

The content exists.

The problem is discovering the right content for the right board, grade, and language.

As a result:

❌ Hours are wasted searching

❌ Resources remain fragmented

❌ Learning becomes inconsistent

❌ High-quality free content stays undiscovered

---

## 💡 Our Solution

TeachFlow AI acts as an intelligent educational discovery agent.

Teachers simply enter:

* Board
* Standard / Grade
* Language Medium
* Topic

The AI then:

1. Expands the query intelligently
2. Searches multiple educational platforms
3. Filters resources by curriculum
4. Categorizes results
5. Delivers everything in one organized workspace

---

            │
            ▼

            │
            ▼
Tavily Search API
            │
            ├── Videos
            ├── Simulations
            ├── PDFs

            ▼
